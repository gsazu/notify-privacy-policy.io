Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026 | App Version: 1.0.8+

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which DNotify Manager ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of our Android application.

100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee

DNotify Manager is built with a zero-compromise, local-first architecture. All notification processing, rule matching, log creation, and network speed tracking occur 100% locally on your device inside an encrypted Room SQLite database. We never transmit, upload, or sell your message contents, notification history, or personal data to any external server.

1. Core Features & Data Processing

a. Notification Listener & Rule Engine

The App utilizes Android's official NotificationListenerService API to detect incoming notifications and compare titles and body text against your custom keyword rules (e.g. "Urgent", "OTP", "Bank Alert").

  • Local Storage: Your custom rules, keyword targets (Title, Body, Both), match types (Contains, Exact, Starts With, Ends With), tone selections, and notification logs are saved strictly in local device memory.
  • App Packages: You can choose whether a rule applies to all installed apps or specific target apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Banking apps). This configuration remains on-device only.
  • No Cloud Sync: Notification message text is processed in real time and discarded or stored in local log history according to your preferences. It is never transmitted over the internet.

b. Live Internet Speed Meter & Data Usage Tracker

The App provides a live status bar internet speed indicator (KB/s or MB/s) and a 30-day daily data usage tracker for Wi-Fi and Mobile Cellular networks.

  • Traffic Metrics: The App reads system bandwidth counters via Android's native TrafficStats and ConnectivityManager APIs.
  • Local Tracking: Daily network usage tallies are saved exclusively in your local database to generate 30-day usage history charts. No network activity logs or URLs are monitored or uploaded.

c. Custom Sound & Tone Player

When a notification rule triggers, the App uses system media framework APIs to play your designated ringtone, custom sound file, or built-in alarm tone with custom volume, vibration, and duration limits (1 to 30 seconds).

2. Permissions Used & Justification

To provide its core functions seamlessly, DNotify Manager requests the following Android permissions:

  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE Notification Access: Required to inspect incoming notification headers and body text for keyword matching.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE Foreground Services:
    • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Ensures uninterrupted 1-second status bar speed updates and real-time notification keyword monitoring.
    • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Allows continuous background sync of daily Wi-Fi and Mobile data usage totals.
    • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Enables continuous playback of custom ringtones/alarm sounds when a notification rule triggers.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS Post Notifications: Used to display the ongoing Internet Speed status bar notification and the one-tap "STOP TUNE" action card.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Audio Access: Allows you to pick custom audio ringtones from your phone's storage.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Boot Auto-Start: Automatically resumes your active notification rules and speed monitor after restarting your phone.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Unrestricted Battery: Recommended to prevent Android OS from putting the notification monitor to sleep when your screen is off.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Permissions: Required to fetch Google AdMob advertisements and measure Wi-Fi/Cellular throughput.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

While notification text and app data are never shared, the App integrates standard, privacy-compliant third-party SDKs to support development and maintain reliability:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash diagnostics and basic app usage statistics (e.g. app opening, screen views) to help us identify bugs and improve performance. No personal message text is ever included in crash reports.
  • Google Play In-App Updates: Checks for official app updates via Google Play Services.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All data stored by DNotify Manager (rules, logs, data usage history, preferences) resides on your physical device. You have full control over your data:

  • You can clear notification log history at any time from the Logs Screen.
  • You can reset or edit rules from the Rules Screen.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all associated databases, preference files, and stored logs from your device immediately.

5. Children's Privacy

DNotify Manager is a general utility application and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.
